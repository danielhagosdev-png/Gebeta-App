# Launch plan: Gebeta on Google Play

## Before submitting
- [x] App icon (`assets/icon.png`, 1024×1024, no AI-generated artwork)
- [x] Feature graphic (`store/feature-graphic.png`, 1024×500)
- [x] Store listing text (`store/LISTING.md`)
- [x] Privacy policy (`store/PRIVACY.md`). Host it at a public URL.
- [x] Native haptic feedback and an in-app rating prompt, to show the app is more than a web page
- [ ] Screenshots: 4 to 8 phone screenshots, taken on a real device or emulator. Suggested shots:
  1. Title screen with the board artwork
  2. Mid-game board with a capture about to happen
  3. Victory or defeat screen
  4. Stats dashboard with match history
  5. Settings showing the board theme and accent choices
  6. Tigrinya or Amharic screen, to show the localization
- [ ] Optional: a 15 to 30 second gameplay video for the listing and social posts
- [ ] Closed testing with at least 20 testers for 14 days (for a new personal account)

## Store listing (visibility)
- Use the full description and keywords in `store/LISTING.md`.
- Add localized listings in Tigrinya and Amharic. Few games in the store offer these, so it helps search and makes the app stand out.
- Pick the Board category and set the content rating carefully.

## Reviews and ratings
- The app asks for a rating after the 3rd finished match, once per install.
- Do not offer rewards, discounts, or anything else for ratings. Google Play bans incentivized reviews, and the account can be suspended.
- Reply to reviews, especially negative ones. Replies show up on the listing.

## Reaching people directly
Most early downloads come from people you reach yourself:
- Share with Ethiopian and Eritrean community groups on Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Instagram, in English, Tigrinya, and Amharic.
- Ask your friends and family to play and leave an honest review.
- Post a short gameplay video, showing the capture rules, which people find interesting to watch.
- Share with teachers and cultural groups. Gebeta is a traditional game, and schools and heritage organizations may want to share it.
- Post in relevant subreddits and board-game communities, following each group's rules about promotion.
- Avoid spam. Post in each group only where it's allowed, and reply to comments.

## Tracking
- Check the Play Console statistics weekly: store listing visitors, installs, and conversion rate.
- Track which channel brings the installs and do more of that.
