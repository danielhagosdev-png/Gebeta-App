**Full description:**
Gebeta (ገበጣ) is a traditional Ethiopian and Eritrean count-and-capture strategy game, played for generations on a carved wooden board.

Sow your stones around the pits, capture your opponent's stones, and outthink your rival. Gebeta is easy to learn and hard to master.

- Authentic Gebeta rules, including family-of-four captures
- Play against the computer on Easy, Medium, or Hard
- Local 2-player mode for head-to-head games
- Interactive 3D board
- Match history and a personal stats dashboard
- Customize the board theme, accent colors, and sowing speed
- English, Tigrinya, and Amharic
- Works offline. No account or sign-in needed.

Gebeta is developed by Mesob Games Solutions.
