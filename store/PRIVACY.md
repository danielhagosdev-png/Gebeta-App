# Privacy Policy: Gebeta

Last updated: [DATE]

Gebeta is developed by Mesob Games Solutions.

## Data we collect
Gebeta does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics.

## Data stored on your device
The game saves your settings (language, board theme, sound on or off, and difficulty) and your local match history and statistics in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Third-party services
The game loads fonts from Google Fonts. Loading those fonts may send your IP address and the browser or app request details to Google, under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children
Gebeta is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same link and the "Last updated" date will change.
